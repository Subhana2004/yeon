02 What we believe

Discovery should inspire curiosity.
Evidence should earn trust.

We're designing YEON to give students a clearer view of real scientific work—without pretending that a data point tells the whole story.

01 /
Sources over guesses

Lab connections and research claims should be traceable, with uncertainty clearly shown.

02 /
Context over rankings

Different labs solve different problems. Explore the work instead of chasing artificial scores.

03 /
People over noise

Build for students and independent researchers who want to understand, compare, and explore.
