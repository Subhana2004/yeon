02 / EVIDENCE BEFORE ASSUMPTIONS

Built to show why.
Not just what.

YEON is an independent project, not an official university directory. Its ambition is to make research discovery more useful without turning uncertainty into fact.

01 /

Lab links need proof.

A university affiliation is not enough to claim a paper belongs to a specific lab. Laboratory relationships will be linked to primary evidence.

02 /

Connections need context.

Coauthored papers can show a research relationship. They do not, on their own, prove a formal partnership between labs.

03 /

Opportunities need dates.

Recruitment information will point to official announcements and display when it was last checked—never promise that a position is open.
